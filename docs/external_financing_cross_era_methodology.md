# Cross-era measurement architecture for Brazil's external financing wedge

## Structural model object
Keep the same equation across the research program:
rp_t = phiD * d_t + eps_rp,t

phiDD is removed from the baseline model.

## Measurement principle
The theoretical object is kept constant while the best historical observable changes when Brazil's external financing regime changes.

## Paper I
Use the marginal syndicated-loan financing wedge: LIBOR minus TB3MS plus Brazil's contractual spread over LIBOR.

## Paper II
After the 1982 debt crisis, normal voluntary bank-loan spreads are not always valid market prices because restructuring and concerted lending can dominate. Use sovereign-bond spreads where available and secondary debt prices as stress evidence; do not manufacture a continuous spread from debt prices without a pricing model.

## Paper III
From the bond-market re-entry / Brady era onward, use market sovereign spreads such as EMBI and Brady/Eurobond spreads.

## Published effective-cost spread
Aiolfi-Catao-Timmermann / Cardoso-Fishlow Ext.Spread is retained as validation of the inherited-debt financing burden. It is not treated as the same object as the marginal syndicated-loan spread.
